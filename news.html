VANTAGE Training Course 2026 — Highlights

The 2026 VANTAGE Training Course took place August 10–21, 2026, bringing together 27 participants from North America, Europe, Asia, Africa, and Latin America. Women accounted for 66.7% of participants, while 37.0% were PhD students. The interdisciplinary cohort spanned public policy, international relations, security studies, economics, and engineering. Following successful completion of the course requirements, the eight highest-performing participants were awarded Certificates of Participation.
